Student Assistant is an app for managing your study schedule and homework.

Features:
- Maintain a semester schedule with the ability to add subjects, teachers, classrooms, and class times
- Support for repeating classes: by days of the week or with flexible week selection (e.g., odd/even weeks only)
- Homework management with deadlines, descriptions, and subject linking
- Multiple schedules support — suitable for changing semesters or parallel programs
- Customizable class start times, lesson durations, and breaks
- Autocomplete for subject names and teachers

The app works completely offline, requires no internet connection, and does not collect user data.

Developed using modern Android architecture principles: Modularity, Jetpack Compose, and safe state management.

The source code is open source — you can study, modify, or contribute to the project.

Designed for students and schoolchildren who want to keep their studies organized without unnecessary complexity.
